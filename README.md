# Mattel Inc.: Determinants of Quarterly Stock Performance (2016–2025)

## Project Overview
This study investigates the key drivers influencing the quarterly stock performance of **Mattel Inc.** (expressed as percentage change in closing stock price) between **Q1 2016 and Q1 2025** (37 quarterly observations).

Through an empirical econometrics framework using a **Multiple Linear Regression Model**, the project evaluates the impact of internal financial indicators, marketing expenditures, consumer interest metrics, and broader market benchmarks.

---

## Key Findings & Methodology
* **Core Model Fit:** The multiple linear regression model explains **64.42% of the variance ($R^2 = 0.6442$)** in quarterly stock performance ($F = 3.203, p = 0.0072$).
* **Key Drivers Identified:**
  * **EBIT & Operational Profitability ($p < 0.05$):** Directly and positively influences quarterly equity returns.
  * **S&P 400 MidCap Benchmark ($p < 0.01$):** Demonstrates a strong positive systemic market co-movement.
  * **NASDAQ Composite Index ($p < 0.10$):** Shows an inverse relationship, suggesting a defensive asset perception by investors during high-tech market shifts.
  * **Google Trends & Marketing Spend:** Analyzed brand visibility ("Mattel", "Barbie") alongside advertising costs to measure consumer interest conversion.
